    Create a word search

    1. Enter a puzzle title.
    2. Enter the words to hide, separated by spaces or new lines. Use dashes to separate multi-word clues.
    3. Set the sharing options for players.
    4. Click Play to generate a shareable word search.

    You can optionally change the grid size or clue display before playing.

    How to play word search

    • Drag in any direction to circle words, or click start and end letters.
    • Edit returns to the generator with this puzzle.
    • Share copies a clean, unplayed puzzle link.
    • Reveal! reveals one unfound word.
    • Reset clears your progress.
    • Save copies a link containing your current progress.
    • Create a New Puzzle opens a blank generator.
